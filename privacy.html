Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

ScanPro is published by Lucian Veghes as an individual developer. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Data we collect

Nothing leaves your device. ScanPro collects no analytics, no identifiers, no crash reports and no advertising data. There is no account and no server-side processing.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan documents and signatures. Photos you pick are read on-device. Scans, signatures, stamps and PDFs are stored in the app's private container until you delete them or remove the app. If you export or share a file, the destination you choose (Files, Mail, another app) handles it under its own policy.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. ScanPro receives only the entitlement (whether Pro is unlocked); Apple handles payment data. See Apple's privacy policy for the store side.

Network

The only network access is local: AirPrint printers and eSCL scanners on your Wi-Fi via Bonjour/mDNS. No document content is uploaded.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to export or erase on our side. Deleting the app removes all locally stored documents. For GDPR questions write to the address below.
